About this book

This bestselling text provides a comprehensive introduction to computer networking through the innovative top-down approach. It balances theory and practice with real-world examples, making complex concepts easy to understand. The 8th Edition includes updated coverage of wireless, mobile, and cloud networking, along with new exercises and review questions.

Widely adopted by academic institutions and networking professionals worldwide, this edition incorporates recent advancements in networking architecture, Software-Defined Networking (SDN), internet security, and modern transport layer protocols including QUIC. Features hands-on Wireshark lab assignments and comprehensive end-of-chapter problems.
